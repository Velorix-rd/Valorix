1. HOW IT WORKS & WHY IT'S 100% SAFE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Most people worry about two things when sending files online:
- "Can someone peek at my photos or videos?"
- "Where is my data being stored?"

Here is how Velorix protects you:

* THE DIGITAL SAFE (AES ENCRYPTION):
  - Imagine putting your file into a heavy steel safe right inside your phone or laptop.
  - Your file gets locked BEFORE it even leaves your screen.
  - The only key to open that safe is hidden inside the link you share with your friend.
  - Even if someone intercepts the link or checks the servers, all they see is scrambled,

  - Instead of uploading your file to a big company's warehouse first, Velorix connects
    an invisible high-speed bridge straight from your phone to your friend's computer.
  - Your file flows directly from you to them.

* EASY & SAFE GOOGLE SIGN-IN:
  - You don't need to invent or remember a new password.
  - Sign in safely with your regular Google account in one click. We never see or
    store your Google password.
